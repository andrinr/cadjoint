THE HERO
  A live capture: playground -> Run -> Simulate -> Solve -> Results, construction
  overlay off (toggle-construction-overlay), slice X at 0.55, zoom 5 detents in.
  The slice is a real inspection mode and the only honest way to show the interior
  of a field whose hot region is an internal boundary — without it the part reads
  as a near-black solid, because that is what the surface temperature is.
  The captured viewport is light paper in BOTH themes: a screenshot does not
  invert, so the readouts drawn over it are fixed to the light ink stack, and the
  plate's edge is stated by a 1px #605e65 rule rather than by luminance.
